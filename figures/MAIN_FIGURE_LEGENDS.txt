Figure 2 | Candidate-aware ECG inference supports concurrent AF identification.
a, The two alternative frozen representation pipelines use the same central five-second raw ECG interval and separate fixed downstream linear-probe/calibration recipes, not an E1/E2 ensemble. B14 denotes demographic/RR/quality covariates, V31 candidate visibility/availability, N6 candidate quantity/matching information and M25 candidate morphology. Original RR/P covariates retain their six-second support; encoder-specific preprocessing differs. The paths are schematic, not a literal layer-count diagram or activation attribution. b,c, All six prespecified E pipelines are shown as existing empirical ROC/PR coordinates with existing shape-preserving display interpolation. Exact predictions, not smoothed curves, define AUROC and AP. Solid/dashed curves distinguish E1/E2; colours identify recipes. The dotted PR line marks 150/2,095 AF prevalence. d, Saved equal-width calibration bins for the four V/N and V/N/M models; original counts accompany the source table and some bins are sparse. This is concurrent-AF probability calibration, not future-risk calibration. e, Distribution of detector-matched candidate fraction for 1,945 AF-negative and 150 AF-positive evaluation ECGs. Half violins show a display density restricted to the observed fraction's valid range, points show every ECG with deterministic jitter, thick bars span quartiles and short dark lines show medians. These are observation-process outputs, not certified P coverage, P absence or atrial biology. f,g, Conditional V/N and morphology changes in average precision and Brier score for both encoders, with existing exploratory pointwise 95% paired patient-cluster intervals. E2 V/N has favourable AP and Brier contrasts; E1 does not establish the same increment, and both conditional morphology contrasts remain inconclusive. All panels concern 2,095 ECGs from 1,822 patients in a previously viewed, source-family-exposed development evaluation; no external-unseen, future-AF or stroke-risk validation is implied. No model fitting, inference or threshold tuning was performed for this figure.
