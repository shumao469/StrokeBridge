# Public data attribution and scope

No raw database files or model weights are redistributed here. Aggregate analyses and public-waveform illustrations retain their source attribution and do not convert a source-exposed dataset into an independent unseen validation cohort.

## PTB-XL

Wagner, P., Strodthoff, N., Bousseljot, R., Samek, W. and Schaeffter, T. (2022). *PTB-XL, a large publicly available electrocardiography dataset*, version 1.0.3. PhysioNet. DOI: [10.13026/kfzx-aw45](https://doi.org/10.13026/kfzx-aw45).

Original publication: Wagner et al. (2020), *PTB-XL, a large publicly available electrocardiography dataset*. Scientific Data. DOI: [10.1038/s41597-020-0495-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0495-6).

The official [version 1.0.3 resource](https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/) lists **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**. Its official 500-Hz waveform release is distinct from the 100-Hz derivative; acquisition frequency and release processing must not be conflated. Concurrent rhythm labels do not establish incident AF/stroke outcomes.

## LUDB

Kalyakulina, A., Yusipov, I., Moskalenko, V., Nikolskiy, A., Kosonogov, K., Zolotykh, N. and Ivanchenko, M. (2021). *Lobachevsky University Electrocardiography Database*, version 1.0.1. PhysioNet. DOI: [10.13026/eegm-h675](https://doi.org/10.13026/eegm-h675).

Original publication: Kalyakulina et al. (2020), *LUDB: A New Open-Access Validation Tool for Electrocardiogram Delineation Algorithms*. IEEE Access 8, 186181–186190. DOI: [10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3029211](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3029211).

The official [version 1.0.1 resource](https://www.physionet.org/content/ludb/1.0.1/) lists **Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0**, not CC BY or a software MIT licence. Public record `data/130` is used only as an attributed illustration of a previously selected mechanism. This does not provide new expert P/no-P truth, an unbiased accuracy sample or clinical risk evidence.

## Other sources and representations

Historical source code may reference other public datasets and model families. Their presence in code is not admission, redistribution permission or proof of independent training exposure. SPH remains quarantined; RDB was not scored as independent V1/no-P evidence. Third-party encoder code/weights retain their original licences, are not bundled, and require their own verified attribution and use review.

Resource access and licence statements were checked against the official pages on 2026-10-25. Repository code rights remain separately described in `NOTICE.md`; data licences do not automatically license project software.
